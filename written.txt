Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
- The get bigger as the two objects get further away.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
- The shared overlapping bounding boxes for each pair of objects changes as they move away and closer to each other.
- No same two objects are stuck in the same bounding box.
Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
- Because they are clipping outside the fixed screen space.
